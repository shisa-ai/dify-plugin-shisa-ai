# Privacy

Shisa AI (Model Provider) sends data directly from the user's Dify workspace to the Shisa AI API to provide the requested model service.

## What is collected and transmitted

Depending on the model used, transmitted data may include:

- LLM prompts, conversation messages, and generation parameters
- Audio uploaded for speech recognition
- Text and voice selections submitted for speech synthesis

## How it is used

Data is transmitted only to perform the operation the user requested. API credentials supplied through Dify's credential configuration are used only to authenticate requests to the Shisa AI API.

## Where it goes

Data is sent to the Shisa AI API. Processing by Shisa AI is governed by the [Shisa AI privacy policy](https://platform.shisa.ai/en/terms/privacy) and [terms](https://platform.shisa.ai/en/terms/conditions).

The plugin itself does not store, log, or share this content with any party other than Shisa AI. For paid API access, Shisa processes user content solely to deliver the requested service and does not use it to train models without explicit written permission; see the Shisa AI privacy policy for details.
